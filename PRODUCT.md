# PRODUCT.md — Tangison Technologies Website

## Product

Tangison Technologies public website. The digital home for Tangison Technologies, an applied AI infrastructure company built in Africa.

## Audience

- Potential clients evaluating AI infrastructure partners in Africa and emerging markets
- Technology leaders seeking applied AI solutions for operational challenges
- Researchers and engineers interested in Tangison's R&D division
- Investors and partners evaluating Tangison's capabilities and positioning
- General public discovering Tangison's brand and mission

## Offer

Applied AI infrastructure: practical, production-ready AI systems designed for African and emerging-market conditions. Not research demos. Not generic SaaS. Tangison builds AI that works where infrastructure is unreliable, data is sparse, and operations need resilience.

## Conversion goal

Enquiry: qualified contact via the contact form or direct email. Secondary: sign up for updates when a newsletter mechanism exists.

## Primary call to action

"Start a conversation" on the contact page. "Get in touch" as a secondary CTA on the home page and service pages.

## Brand positioning

Tangison is not "next-generation" or "cutting-edge" AI. Tangison is applied AI that works. Built in Africa for conditions that break generic infrastructure. Reliable, practical, production-grade. The website must communicate competence, restraint, and depth, not hype.

## Build mode

Full build. Every approved route unlocked and functional. No demo locks, no placeholders, no fake data.

## Integrations

- Contact form: email to contact@tangison.com (production mode)
- No authentication required for public pages
- No database required (static site with form submission)
- No analytics until explicitly approved

## Domains

- Primary: tangison.com
- Subdomains: studio.tangison.com (external), agent.tangison.com (future), labs.tangison.com (future)

## Accessibility and compliance

- WCAG 2.1 AA minimum
- Semantic HTML, ARIA labels, keyboard navigation
- Sufficient colour contrast in both themes
- Reduced-motion support mandatory
- Namibian business English, no em dashes, no generic AI language
